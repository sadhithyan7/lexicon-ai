# LEXICON AI — ART DIRECTION

## Personality

Lexicon AI should feel:

- intellectual
- precise
- editorial
- calm
- trustworthy
- technical
- mature

It should feel closer to a professional research/document
workspace than a futuristic AI startup.

---

# VISUAL LANGUAGE

Use:

- editorial typography
- restrained neutral colors
- subtle borders
- strong alignment
- controlled whitespace
- structured information
- compact controls
- functional iconography
- subtle interaction states

---

# AVOID

Do not create:

- futuristic AI interfaces
- cyberpunk aesthetics
- glowing UI
- neon
- purple/blue gradients
- glassmorphism
- floating AI orbs
- particle effects
- excessive rounded cards
- decorative AI graphics
- emoji-based UI

---

# DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The interface should look good when all decoration is removed.

If the page only looks good because of gradients,
glows, shadows or illustrations, the underlying design is weak.

---

# REFERENCES

Use professional software products and editorial interfaces
as conceptual references.

Take inspiration from:

- strong typography
- information hierarchy
- navigation
- grids
- spacing
- restrained color

Do not copy branding or layouts.
