# COMPONENT RULES

## GENERAL

Every component must have a clear purpose.

Do not create components simply because they make the page
look visually richer.

---

## CARDS

Before creating a card ask:

"Does this content require a separate container?"

If not, prefer:

- whitespace
- divider
- list
- table
- section

---

## TABLES

Use tables for structured data.

Do not convert structured data into cards simply for visual styling.

---

## BADGES

Use badges only for:

- status
- category
- priority
- state

Do not use decorative badges.

---

## ICONS

Use Lucide.

Do not mix icon libraries.

Do not use emojis.

---

## BUTTONS

Primary:

one clear primary action.

Secondary:

less visually prominent.

Avoid multiple competing primary buttons.

---

## NAVIGATION

Navigation must be simple and predictable.

Do not use decorative navigation elements.

---

## FORMS

Use:

Label
Input
Helper text
Validation
Action

Do not hide important information behind unnecessary interaction.

---

## DASHBOARDS

Prioritize:

metrics
tables
activity
filters
status

Avoid creating a grid of cards for every piece of information.
